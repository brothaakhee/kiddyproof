How it works

Three seconds in. One gesture out.

No setup, no profiles, no lockout screens. The protection is invisible and instant.

1

Press the shortcut

Hit ⌃⌥⌘K or click the menu-bar shield. Kid mode arms in a single beat — and only after it proves the keyboard is truly captured.

2

The cage drops

A gentle shield settles over the whole screen. Every tap, click, and scroll is caught before it can reach a single app, file, or message.

3

Play becomes light

Your child mashes away and the screen answers in calm, glowing motion. To leave, you hold the ⌃⌥⌘ keys — or press & hold the bottom-left corner — for three seconds. Random mashing can't reproduce a deliberate three-second hold.

The serious part

Engineered like it matters. Because it does.

This isn't a flimsy screen you can click through. It catches every keystroke and click before it can reach your apps — and it was stress-tested against every way it could fail before it ever guarded a screen.

Caught at the source

Every keystroke, click, and scroll is caught the instant it happens — none of it ever reaches your apps. The glowing overlay is just for show.

Your screen, untouched

The cage is transparent at full brightness — nothing dimmed, moved, or closed. Kids love the familiar desktop, and you decide the moment to hand it over.

Only you can leave

Exit needs a deliberate ⌃⌥⌘ hold or a three-second bottom-left corner-hold — gestures a child won't stumble into.

A built-in safety net

A separate little guardian keeps watch the whole time. If anything ever hiccups — even kiddyproof itself — it hands control straight back to you within seconds.

Things we can't protect you from

kiddyproof handles the typing, clicking, and scrolling beautifully. A few things are simply out of any app's hands — so here they are, plainly, because you should never have to guess.

  • The power button. A determined little thumb can still put the Mac to sleep. That ends playtime — but your work stays safe and sound, right where you left it.
  • Extremely clever children. If yours has already memorized your three-second exit gesture… we're as impressed as you are. (Maybe pick a new one.)
  • A whole palm on the trackpad. A big two-fisted swipe can occasionally slip past to another screen. Rare — but toddlers are nothing if not creative.
  • Spilled juice and sticky fingers. kiddyproof guards the software, not the hardware. Keep the snacks — and the full sippy cups — at a safe distance.
  • The laptop walking off. A closed lid still sleeps the Mac, and small hands love to carry things. If you're stepping away, take it with you.
